Privacy you can understand

Local processing, private by default

ClarityRead is built around browser-based reading tools. By default, page content is processed locally for reader mode, visual adjustments, and read-aloud controls instead of being collected or sent to ClarityRead servers.

  • No page-content collection by default.
  • Reader controls run in your browser.
  • Clear privacy information is available before you install.
