Privacy you can understand

Local processing, private by default

ClarityRead is built around browser-based reading tools. By default, page content and selections are processed locally for reader mode, visual adjustments, summarization, and read-aloud controls instead of being collected or sent to remote servers.

  • No page-content collection by default.
  • Reader controls run in your browser.
  • Clear privacy information is available before you install.

Turn cluttered, long-form pages into a focused reading column with adjustable font sizes and line height.

Read-aloud support

High contrast

Boost contrast or invert colors to reduce squinting and make hard-to-see pages more comfortable.
