Quick troubleshooting

  • Ensure the extension is enabled in chrome://extensions.
  • Try disabling other reader or accessibility extensions that may conflict.
  • If read-aloud fails, check browser speech voices and permissions.
🔒

Privacy-first diagnostics

Automatic console logs are disabled by default to protect privacy.

  • Reproduction steps — include URL, steps, expected result, and actual result.
  • Screenshots — capture the problem and extension UI when helpful.
